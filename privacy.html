Information you provide

You can manually mark Sprite variants as owned, record levels, and mark them as mastered. The tracker stores these selections in your browser's local storage on your device. Guest progress is not uploaded to an account. If you sign in, changes to your account collection are sent to the account-storage service described below.

Sprite Tracker accounts

Optional Sprite Tracker accounts use Supabase for email-and-password authentication and online collection storage. When you create an account or sign in, your email address and password are submitted directly to Supabase over an encrypted connection. Account sessions are stored in this browser so you can stay signed in.

Your signed-in collection stores Sprite identifiers, levels, mastery choices, update times, and revision numbers under your account identifier. Database access rules restrict a collection to its signed-in owner. Guest collections stay separate unless you choose to add guest progress to your account. Account saving does not retrieve in-game Fortnite inventory.

The account library is loaded from jsDelivr. This provider and Supabase may receive ordinary connection data, such as IP address and browser details, when your browser contacts them. See Supabase's privacy policy for its practices.

Public Sprite catalog

The site requests public Sprite catalog information, including Sprite names and images, from a third-party Fortnite data API (prod.fn-api.cc). When your browser contacts this provider, the provider may receive ordinary connection data such as your IP address and browser details under its own practices.

Hosting and technical information

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard web request information to serve the website. The tracker does not currently use its own analytics or advertising trackers. Optional accounts and online collections are stored with Supabase.

Storage and deletion

Your collection selections remain in local browser storage until you clear the site's browser data or remove them using the tracker controls. Clearing browser data may permanently erase guest progress or account edits that have not finished saving online. Successfully saved account progress remains online until removed.

To request deletion of a Sprite Tracker account and its online collection, contact the address below from your account email. Signing out does not delete an account or its collection.

Sharing

The tracker does not intentionally sell or share your manually entered collection data with advertisers. External services used to host the site and display public Sprite data operate independently.

Account security

Never submit passwords or access tokens through this website or its feedback channels.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as new features are introduced. The date above will be changed when material updates are made.

Contact

For privacy questions, personal data inquiries, or requests to access or delete information associated with this website, contact spritetracker247@gmail.com. Please do not email passwords or access tokens.

For general questions or bug reports, you may also open an issue on the project's GitHub issue tracker. Do not post private account information in public issues.
