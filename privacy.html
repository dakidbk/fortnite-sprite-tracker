Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Fortnite Sprite Tracker is an independent, fan-made collection tracking website. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Epic Games.

Information you provide

You can manually mark Sprite variants as owned, record levels, and mark them as mastered. The tracker stores these selections in your browser's local storage on your device. This information is not automatically sent to the site's developer or uploaded to a user account.

Epic Games account sign-in

Epic Games sign-in is not currently active on this website. If it is enabled in the future, the site will request permission through Epic Account Services before accessing any authorized Epic account information. Information requested may include your Epic account identifier and display name, depending on the permissions you approve. This policy will be updated before any additional account-data processing is introduced.

Signing in with Epic does not itself provide access to Fortnite inventory, Sprite ownership, levels, or mastery data. Automatic account collection syncing is not currently available.

Public Sprite catalog

The site requests public Sprite catalog information, including Sprite names and images, from a third-party Fortnite data API (prod.fn-api.cc). When your browser contacts this provider, the provider may receive ordinary connection data such as your IP address and browser details under its own practices.

Hosting and technical information

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard web request information to serve the website. The tracker does not currently use its own analytics, advertising trackers, or server-side account database.

Storage and deletion

Your collection selections remain in local browser storage until you clear the site's browser data or remove them using the tracker controls. Clearing browser data may permanently erase your locally saved collection.

Sharing

The tracker does not intentionally sell or share your manually entered collection data with advertisers. External services used to host the site and display public Sprite data operate independently.

Children and account security

Use Epic Games sign-in only through Epic's official authorization page. Never enter an Epic Games password directly into this fan-made tracker. Parents or guardians should supervise children's use of online account services as appropriate.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as new features are introduced. The date above will be changed when material updates are made.

Contact

For privacy questions, personal data inquiries, or requests to access or delete information associated with this website, contact spritetracker247@gmail.com. Please do not email passwords or access tokens.

For general questions or bug reports, you may also open an issue on the project's GitHub issue tracker. Do not post private account information in public issues.
